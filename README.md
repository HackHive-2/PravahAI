# 🌊 PravahAI

> AI-Assisted Hyperlocal Flood Intelligence & Risk-Aware Decision System

PravahAI is a disaster intelligence platform designed to support hyperlocal flood awareness, citizen reporting, emergency response, and risk-informed decision-making.

Built with the vision of making flood intelligence more accessible and actionable, PravahAI brings together geospatial risk information, citizen-generated evidence, emergency facilities, official alerts, and disaster response workflows into a single interface.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🗺️ Hyperlocal Risk Map
- 100m hydro-cartographic risk grid
- Localized flood risk visualization
- Hydrological factor analysis
- Catchment-level risk insights

### 🧭 Route Planner
- Risk-aware route planning
- Identification of exposed roads and flood-prone areas
- Safer mobility support during flood situations

### 📸 Citizen Flood Reporting
- Citizen-generated flood reports
- AI-assisted evidence validation
- Location and field evidence tracking

### 🏥 Emergency Locations
- Verified relief centres and shelters
- Hospitals and medical facilities
- Community shelters
- Facility capacity and operational information

### 📢 Official Alerts
- Multi-source disaster alerts
- Alert prioritization and monitoring

### 🚨 Emergency Command Centre
- Critical and high-risk zone monitoring
- Citizen report verification queue
- Priority action areas
- Exposed road monitoring
- Operational disaster response dashboard

### 📊 Data & Methodology
- Overview of data sources
- Risk modelling methodology
- Hydrological and spatial intelligence factors
